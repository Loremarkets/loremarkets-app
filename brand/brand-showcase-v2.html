01 — Pre-Design Review (Archive)
Carl × Tom Feedback Brief
C
Carl
UX / UI Design Lead
  • Single Bézier arc, not a jagged multi-point line. One confident curve reads better at every size and communicates market momentum more elegantly.
  • Use clipPath on the hex. In v1, bloom and glow elements bled outside the badge outline. That's sloppy at production scale. Clip everything.
  • 3D text extrusion → crisp drop-shadow. The dark-offset text copy in v1 causes muddy rendering on OLED and at zoom. Replace with feDropShadow stdDeviation="1".
  • Book: symbolic, not illustrated. Two curved wing-pages + gold spine = immediately readable at 32px. The ruling lines, runes, and bar chart on pages are invisible at anything below 128px — cut them.
  • Arcane Violet (#8050d0) fails WCAG AA for normal text (4.28:1 vs 4.5:1 required). Flag: use only for decorative/large elements — never for body copy or labels below 18px.
  • Oracle Gold #f5c030 on Void Black: 12.63:1 — WCAG AAA ✓
  • Chart Teal #00ecd6 on Void Black: 13.84:1 — WCAG AAA ✓
  • Minimum clear space: 1× badge width on all sides for full lockup. 0.5× for icon-only use. Do not break this — especially in the nav bar.
  • Favicon variant needed: At 16×16 the grimoire detail is gone. Create a dedicated micro mark: solid hex badge with teal centre-dot only. No book, no arc.
T
Tom
Marketing Lead
  • "TRADE THE LEGEND" is the tagline. It belongs as a tertiary element below the lockup — not inside the logo frame. Logos don't contain taglines.
  • Three use-case lockups, not one. We need: (A) Horizontal Primary for website/email, (B) Modern Unity for social/app store, (C) Badge Authority for favicon/merch. Different contexts, same DNA.
  • Gold = ancient knowledge (Lore). Teal = live data (Markets). This palette split tells our story visually. Enforce it religiously — don't let teal bleed into the logomark's primary text or gold bleed into chart elements.
  • Not crypto, not fintech. Our risk: gold + dark purple reads as DeFi to some audiences. The teal chart arc is our differentiator — it signals simulation and game. Lean into it in all marketing contexts.
  • The grimoire metaphor is correct. Book = Lore. Chart = Markets. Every touchpoint should reinforce this visual story. Don't drift from it in sub-brand assets.
  • Social profile test: The icon at 40×40 on Twitter must be instantly recognisable as LoreMarkets — not a generic badge. The teal glow in the upper portion (the chart apex star) is our unique identifier at small sizes. Protect it.
  • Copy hook tied to the icon: "The most dangerous trades are the ones written in lore." — this is ad copy that our icon visually supports. The brand is cohesive.

03 — Logo Proposals (Archive)
Three Lockups — Vote Closed · Logo Finalised
Proposal A — The Duality
★ Recommended Primary

Classic horizontal lockup. LORE commands in gold. MARKETS grounds in teal. The duality of the brand name is the visual story — you can read the split in a single glance. The thin gold rule and tight tracking on MARKETS creates hierarchy without competition.

Best for: website header · email header · presentation title slide · OpenGraph
Team votes
Proposal B — Modern Unity
Recommended Social

Single-word treatment with a colour-split technique. LORE in gold flows seamlessly into MARKETS in teal — one brand name, two worlds, zero explanation. The diamond separator is a lore artefact. Cleaner at smaller sizes than Proposal A.

Best for: social media profile · app store listing · mobile splash · press kit
Team votes
Proposal C — Badge Authority
Recommended Print / Merch

The icon is the hero. The wordmark is secondary — tracked wide and subordinate. Ceremonial and authoritative. Works brilliantly on dark merchandise, tournament badges, and Discord server icons where the square/circular format is dominant.

Best for: merchandise · tournament assets · Discord icon · game loading screen
Team votes

TEAM RECOMMENDATION

Proposal A is the primary lockup — 3/3 votes. It has the clearest hierarchy, the strongest dual-brand storytelling, and works across the widest range of use cases. Proposal B ships as the social/mobile variant — it compresses better and the gradient-split is a visual hook in crowded feeds. Proposal C is the merch/game-context variant. All three should be in the brand asset kit.
