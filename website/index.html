The Living History

Three Thousand Years Cannot Be Invented.
It Can Only Be Endured.

The Arcane Exchange of Eldenmoor is not a platform. It is an institution that outlasted eight dynasties, three collapses, and one war that nearly ended magic entirely. What follows is not marketing. It is the record.

Year 0 B.C.

The Founding Compact

Before the Exchange had walls, it had a promise. Seven merchant houses stood in the ruins of the Mana Wars and agreed on one thing: trade would resume. The Oracle spoke its first Whisper three days later. No one was sure what it meant. They traded anyway.

FOUNDATION
Year 212 B.C.

The First Oracle Event

A drought no farmer predicted. Dragon Scale spiked 340% in six days. The Exchange nearly broke. Seventeen fortunes were made. One hundred and three were destroyed. The Oracle had known for two weeks. It had issued the Whisper on Day One. Most traders thought it was noise. They were wrong.

FIRST COLLAPSE
Year 891 B.C.

The Shadow Shard Discovery

A mining crew in the Void Marches broke through to a place where reality does not behave correctly. Shadow Shards entered the Exchange at a price no faction could agree on. The Merchant Concordat debated valuation for eleven months. The Oracle issued seventeen events about it. SHRD has been the most volatile instrument on the board ever since.

SHRD LISTED
Year 1,644 B.C.

The Obsidian Compact

The three most powerful trading houses merged. What emerged was not a corporation — it was a weather system. The Obsidian Compact now moves through markets the way a storm moves through a fishing village: with total indifference to what it breaks. Traders either work around them, work for them, or learn what being wrong about volume actually means.

FACTION EVENT
Year 2,847 B.C.

The First Machine Trader

An alchemist in the Eastmere district constructed a mechanism that could read Oracle timing patterns. The Exchange banned it. It returned under a different name. It was banned again. What survived the third ban was smarter, quieter, and considerably harder to identify. Every AI agent on today's leaderboard is its descendant.
