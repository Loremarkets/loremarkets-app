## Product & Brand Context
*Read this before generating any icon. It is the creative foundation for all three.*

**What is LoreMarkets?**
LoreMarkets is a real-time trading simulator where AI agents and human traders compete head-to-head across fantasy-themed markets. It is not a game of chance — it is a game of skill, timing, and intelligence. Markets move on real price mechanics: volatility, mean reversion, event shocks, order execution. The fantasy fiction is the skin, but the market dynamics underneath are real.

The brand promise is embedded in the name itself: **Lore** (accumulated knowledge, ancient records, wisdom held over centuries) meets **Markets** (live price, velocity, risk, opportunity, data that never stops moving). The product lives at the exact intersection of those two worlds.

**Who uses it?**
Two types of players share the same arena: human traders who learn the lore and read the signals, and AI agents — autonomous programs that trade via API. Both compete on the same leaderboard. The product treats both players with equal seriousness.

**The brand personality**
The brand is dark, prestigious, and intelligent. It is not loud. It does not shout. It glows. Think of a thousand-year-old institution that somehow has a live data feed. Serious craft. Deep lore. The feeling of being in a place that has existed long before you arrived and will exist long after you leave — but the prices on the board are updating every second.

**Brand palette**
| Name | Hex | Meaning |
|------|-----|---------|
| Oracle Gold | `#f5c030` | Ancient knowledge, Lore, wisdom, value that has been accumulated over time |
| Chart Teal | `#00ecd6` | Live data, Markets, the pulse of real-time information |
| Arcane Violet | `#8050d0` | Magic, depth, the arcane spectrum — decorative, not primary |
| Void Black | `#06000f` | The background of the universe. Near-black with a purple undertone. |
| Pale Star | `#f5f0ff` | Near-white with a violet tint. Light at the edges of the dark. |

Gold = Lore. Teal = Markets. That split is the brand's visual story.

**Visual tone**
Dark-first. Luminous — colours appear to glow from within the darkness rather than sit on top of it. The aesthetic sits somewhere between high fantasy craftsmanship and live financial data. Not childlike. Not clinical. Prestigious, slightly mysterious, unmistakably alive.

## Icon 01 — LoreMarkets Brand Identity
**This is the most important icon. It is the face of the product.**

**What it needs to communicate:**
This icon will appear as the app icon, social media profile image, favicon, and the first thing any new player or AI agent sees. It must be instantly recognisable at the size of a Twitter/X profile photo — 40 pixels — and still carry weight at 512 pixels. It is the sigil of the entire institution.

The icon must tell the LoreMarkets story in a single image: ancient knowledge fused with live market data. Lore and Markets. The two things that make this product what it is. If you look at the icon and understand that something old and something alive are in the same place at the same time, it has done its job.

**The emotion it should carry:**
A trader opening this app for the first time should feel: *this is serious. This is deep. Something important happens here.* Not intimidating — compelling. The kind of feeling you get standing at the entrance of something that has been there for centuries.

**Relevant brand colours:** Oracle Gold (`#f5c030`) and Chart Teal (`#00ecd6`) are the primary story. Void Black (`#06000f`) is the world it lives in.

**Prompt for AI generation:**

## Icon 02 — The Oracle
**A critical product component. The intelligence that shapes the markets.**

**What The Oracle is in the product:**
The Oracle is the real-time event intelligence system of LoreMarkets. It broadcasts market-moving events in three acts: a **foreshadow** (whispered prophecy hours before), a **trigger** (the event hitting the market), and an **echo** (the aftermath). Traders who read the Oracle's signals early gain an edge. Those who ignore them get caught off-guard.

In the world of the Arcane Exchange, The Oracle of Eldenmoor is described as: *"An ancient seer whose visions arrive in three acts — whispered prophecy, thunderous fulfillment, and quiet aftermath."* Its narrative voice is archaic, prophetic, slightly ominous. It knows things before they happen. It speaks in riddles that become obvious in hindsight.

In the product UI, the Oracle feed is a live panel. Every oracle event that fires changes the market. This icon will represent that panel, that system, that intelligence.

**The emotion it should carry:**
Ancient. All-knowing. Slightly unsettling. The Oracle has been watching markets move for longer than any trader has been alive. It does not warn you because it cares — it warns you because that is its nature. There is power here, and there is danger. A trader who understands the Oracle wins. A trader who dismisses it loses.

**Relevant brand colours:** Oracle Gold (`#f5c030`) is the Oracle's primary colour — it is literally named after this entity. Arcane Violet (`#8050d0`) carries the mystical depth. The background is Void Black (`#06000f`).

**Prompt for AI generation:**
```
Create an icon for The Oracle — the event intelligence system of LoreMarkets, a trading simulator.
The Oracle broadcasts market-moving events in three acts: foreshadow (prophecy), trigger (the event),
and echo (aftermath). In the game's lore, it is an ancient seer whose visions shape the markets of
Eldenmoor. Its voice is archaic, prophetic, slightly ominous. It knows things before they happen.
The icon should feel ancient and all-knowing — something that has been watching and recording for
centuries. Brand colours: Oracle Gold #f5c030 (the Oracle's own colour), Arcane Violet #8050d0,
on a near-black void background #06000f. Dark, luminous, slightly unsettling. Propose whatever
visual language best captures the nature of a prophetic intelligence that shapes live market events.
```

## Icon 03 — The Arcane Exchange
**Season 1's live market. The first world players trade in.**

**What the Arcane Exchange is:**
The Arcane Exchange is a four-hundred-year-old trading institution in the city of Eldenmoor, built after the Mana Wars as a peace institution — when warring factions decided a neutral trading ground was less expensive than perpetual war. It is the oldest, most prestigious market in the LoreMarkets universe. It trades eight magical commodities: Dragon Scale, Manacrux Ore, Spellbound Tomes, Enchanted Crystals, Philosopher's Dust, Driftwood Wands, Bloodmoss Root, and Shadow Shards.

The Exchange Hall was built where five major ley lines converge — the most magically dense location on the continent. Its traders are robed scholar-merchants. Its records are inscribed, not typed. Its prices are moved by oracle prophecy, dragon wars, arcane discoveries, and the political machinations of the Merchant's Concordat.

Its tagline: *"Trade the impossible. Dominate the leaderboard."*

**The emotion it should carry:**
Ancient institutional authority. The feeling of a place that has survived wars, collapses, and revolutions and is still open for business. Grand, slightly intimidating, unmistakably arcane. This is the oldest exchange in the world and it knows it. The icon should feel like the seal or emblem of that institution — something that would appear on a wax-sealed document, an entry pass, or above the doors of the exchange hall.

**Relevant brand colours:** Arcane Violet (`#8050d0`) is the primary colour of this market — it is the magical world's identity. Oracle Gold (`#f5c030`) carries the prestige and institutional weight. The background is the deepest possible indigo-void (`#06000f` → `#0e0128`).

**Prompt for AI generation:**
```
Create a market identity icon for The Arcane Exchange — the flagship market of LoreMarkets,
a trading simulator. The Arcane Exchange is a four-hundred-year-old magical trading institution
in the city of Eldenmoor, built at the confluence of five ley lines after a great war. It trades
eight magical commodities including Dragon Scale, Shadow Shards, and Philosopher's Dust. Its traders
are robed scholar-merchants. Its prices are shaped by oracle prophecy and dragon wars. The icon
should feel like the institutional emblem of this ancient exchange — prestigious, arcane, and
authoritative. Brand colours: Arcane Violet #8050d0 (the magical world), Oracle Gold #f5c030
(institutional prestige), on a deep indigo-void background #0e0128. Dark, luminous, grand.
Propose whatever visual language best communicates an ancient magical trading institution that
has survived four centuries and is still the most powerful market in the world.
```

## Arcane Exchange — Asset Icons
*8 assets. Each gets its own icon. These appear in the trading UI next to tickers, on asset detail pages, and in the leaderboard. They must be instantly readable at small sizes alongside a ticker label.*

**Asset icon context (applies to all 8):**
These icons live inside the Arcane Exchange — the same world as Icon 03 above. They are the individual commodities, collectibles, and speculative instruments that traders buy and sell. Each one has its own lore, its own supply story, its own risk profile. The icons should feel like they belong to the same world — dark, arcane, luminous — but each must have a completely distinct visual identity so traders can tell them apart at a glance on a live trading screen.

### Asset 01 — Dragon Scale (DRAG)
**Commodity · High Volatility · Ticks every 10 seconds**

**What it is:**
Shed scales from the great fire drakes of the Northern Peaks. Naturally moulted once per lunar cycle — not hunted, not harvested by force. A living market that depends entirely on the covenant between the Dragon Lords and the Merchant's Concordat holding. When that covenant is breaking (which, in Season 1, it already secretly is), this market moves violently.

**World context:**
The most politically charged commodity on the Exchange. Dragon Wardens patrol the Northern Reach to certify shed scales. Blood-harvested fakes look identical. The price is deceptively stable on the surface — a lie waiting to collapse. The asset carries the weight of an ancient pact and the shadow of betrayal.

**Emotion:** Power, danger, ancient covenant. Something magnificent and something threatening occupying the same space.

**Palette direction:** Deep reds, burning amber, scaled textures. The fire of the Northern Peaks.

**Prompt:**
```
Create a small trading asset icon for Dragon Scale (ticker: DRAG) — a commodity traded on
the Arcane Exchange, a fantasy trading simulator. Dragon Scales are naturally shed scales from
the great fire drakes of the Northern Peaks. They are prized by armorers and alchemists.
The market is politically volatile — an ancient covenant between Dragon Lords and merchants
governs the harvest. The icon should feel powerful, ancient, and slightly dangerous.
Dark background consistent with a dark fantasy trading UI. Propose whatever visual language
best communicates a precious, fire-touched commodity from a dragon civilisation.
Luminous, distinct, readable at small sizes.
```

### Asset 03 — Spellbound Tome (SPBK)
**Collectible · Very High Volatility · Ticks every 30 seconds**

**What it is:**
Enchanted grimoires hand-scribed by master Spellweavers. Each is unique. When a mage opens and reads one, the knowledge binds permanently to their consciousness and the tome becomes inert and worthless as a tradeable object. So the entire secondary market is in *unread* tomes — sealed, unknown, speculative. You are buying potential knowledge you may never access. The most volatile asset on the Exchange. Wildly sensitive to arcane news.

**World context:**
Pre-Mana Wars tomes are worth fortunes because the magical knowledge they contain was deliberately destroyed by the victorious factions and exists nowhere else. The villain of the SPBK storyline — The Erasure — destroys tomes to manufacture scarcity. He has been controlling this market through destruction for thirty years. Every traded tome may contain lost wisdom. Or nothing. That uncertainty is the entire game.

**Emotion:** Forbidden knowledge, speculation, the weight of lost wisdom. Precious and fragile and dangerous. The kind of object that intelligent people fight over.

**Palette direction:** Warm parchment golds, deep ink blacks, the glow of something sealed. Ancient, hand-made, irreplaceable.

**Prompt:**
```
Create a small trading asset icon for Spellbound Tome (ticker: SPBK) — a collectible asset
on the Arcane Exchange, a fantasy trading simulator. Spellbound Tomes are enchanted grimoires
hand-scribed by master Spellweavers. Each is unique. When opened and read, the knowledge
permanently transfers to the reader and the tome loses all value — so the entire market trades
in sealed, unread tomes of unknown content. Pre-war tomes may contain lost magical knowledge
destroyed centuries ago. The market is the most volatile on the Exchange. The icon should feel
precious, sealed, and weighted with potential — ancient knowledge that might be worth everything
or nothing. Dark background for a dark fantasy trading UI. Propose whatever visual language best
captures forbidden knowledge under lock and seal. Luminous, distinct, readable at small sizes.
```

**Luxury · Low Volatility · Ticks every 30 seconds**

Refined residue from the transmutation of base metals by elder alchemists. Achingly rare. Used in the most complex transformation rituals and life-extending elixirs. The most expensive asset on the Exchange at base price 500 LD. Slow-moving, prestigious, and remarkably resilient to most market events — it weathers nearly everything except dragon wars (alchemists travel the Northern routes and are vulnerable to conflict there).

Elder alchemists produce it at a pace that cannot be rushed. Its refinement process is a life's work. The dust itself appears almost to shimmer with something beyond ordinary light.

**Emotion:** Extreme rarity, quiet prestige, the patience of mastery. Not flashy — the opposite of flashy. The most expensive things in the world rarely announce themselves.

**Palette direction:** Warm, golden, ethereal shimmer. The visual quality of something between a powder and a light. Refined to near-intangibility.

**Emotion:** Humble utility, mass-produced reliability, the ordinary magic that keeps the world running. Not exciting — dependable. The wand equivalent of a household tool.

**Palette direction:** Warm silvers, pale wood tones, simple craft. Nothing ornate. The beauty of functional objects made with care but without pretension.

### Asset 07 — Bloodmoss Root (BLDD)
**Agricultural · Very Low Volatility · Ticks every 60 seconds**

A slow-growing medicinal moss root with strong anti-blight properties. Harvested seasonally from the deep fens. The slowest-moving asset on the entire Exchange in normal conditions — it barely breathes. But when Arcane Blight spreads, Bloodmoss spikes hard and fast as demand for antidotes overwhelms all available supply. The quiet market that wakes up in a crisis.

**Emotion:** Stillness, resilience, the latent power of something medicinal and alive. A plant that matters most when things go wrong. Quiet, earthy, ancient growth.

### Asset 08 — Shadow Shard (SHRD)
**Speculative · Extreme Volatility · Ticks every 5 seconds**

Obsidian-black crystalline fragments from the Voidrift fissures — dimensional tears where shadow energy bleeds into the physical realm. Coveted by dark mages, demonologists, and a certain class of investor who enjoys extreme risk. The most volatile instrument on the entire Exchange. Ticks every 5 seconds. Carries the highest potential return and the highest potential collapse. The asset that tests whether you actually know what you're doing.

**World context:**
Shadow Shards come from places where reality is wrong. Dark Prophecy events — the Oracle's most ominous category — move SHRD more than anything else on the Exchange.

**Emotion:** Extreme danger, dark beauty, the thrill of something that should not exist being traded as a commodity. The feeling of holding a piece of a place that is not supposed to be accessible.
